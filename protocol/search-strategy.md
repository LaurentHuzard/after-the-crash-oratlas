# Search strategy

Search cutoff: 2026-08-06.

## Core concept blocks

### Exposure

```
amphetamine OR dexamphetamine OR dextroamphetamine OR
"mixed amphetamine salts" OR lisdexamfetamine OR
methamphetamine OR "amphetamine-type stimulant"
```

### Withdrawal and recovery

```
withdrawal OR abstinence OR cessation OR crash OR post-acute OR protracted OR
sleep OR fatigue OR anhedonia OR depression OR craving OR cognition OR functioning
```

### Design and intervention

```
cohort OR longitudinal OR prospective OR trial OR randomized OR systematic review OR
meta-analysis OR guideline OR psychometric OR treatment
```

## Targeted searches

1. `(amphetamine terms) AND (withdrawal terms)`
2. `amphetamine withdrawal sleep`
3. `amphetamine withdrawal questionnaire validation`
4. `methamphetamine withdrawal trajectory`
5. `methamphetamine withdrawal pharmacological treatment systematic review`
6. `amphetamine-type stimulant use disorder meta-analysis`
7. backward citation chasing from the 2009 Cochrane review and 2023 clinical review

## Screening notes

Titles were not trusted as substance labels. Abstracts and methods were checked for the actual
exposure whenever accessible. Cocaine-only evidence was excluded from primary claims and
retained only where it explained measurement or psychosocial extrapolation.
