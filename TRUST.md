# TRUST assessment policy

This pilot contains 77 relation-level TRUST records, exactly one for each
claim–citation relation.

## What the ratings mean

Ratings are ordinal evaluations of one source in relation to one qualified claim. They are not:

- probabilities that a paper is true;
- quality scores for an entire publication;
- clinical recommendations;
- substitutes for independent human review.

## Criteria

1. identity integrity;
2. entailment;
3. source access;
4. population relevance;
5. intervention or exposure relevance;
6. outcome relevance;
7. methodological safeguards;
8. statistical safeguards;
9. replication convergence;
10. conflict dependency.
