# Evidence gaps and the next useful studies

(claim-c12)=
## C12 · Recovery is larger than a negative test

**Claim.** Functional recovery outcomes such as return to work, daily organization,
relationships, cognition, energy, pleasure, and quality of life are under-measured relative to
abstinence, toxicology, craving, and retention.

Large syntheses mainly report abstinence, urine results, craving, and dropout
[@khalili2025; @sharafi2024; @decrescenzo2018]. Major medication trials use toxicology or days
of use as primary outcomes [@trivedi2021; @mcketin2026]. The 2026 mirtazapine trial included
quality of life, depression, and insomnia, but these were secondary and did not significantly
improve [@mcketin2026]. Research-priority work still calls for broader implementation and
population evidence [@ballester2025].

## Highest-value next studies

1. **A prospective non-methamphetamine amphetamine cohort.** Daily or near-daily measurement
   during the first month, followed by monthly functional outcomes to one year.
2. **A common outcome core.** Withdrawal severity, craving, sleep, mood, cognition, quality of
   life, return to roles, adverse events, and use outcomes reported separately.
3. **Substance verification.** Chemical or toxicological characterization where street
   “amphetamine” composition is uncertain.
4. **Comorbidity-aware trajectories.** Repeated assessment of bipolar disorder, depression,
   psychosis, ADHD, trauma, and polysubstance use rather than one baseline exclusion checkbox.
5. **Recovery meaningful to participants.** Outcomes co-designed with people who have lived
   experience, including energy, pleasure, relationships, dignity, and practical daily life.
6. **Intervention trials with explicit targets.** A trial should state whether it aims to reduce
   acute withdrawal, prevent return to use, reduce overall use, improve functioning, or several
   separately powered outcomes.

## Pilot conclusion

The evidence is not a blank page. It is a palimpsest: old direct amphetamine studies under
newer methamphetamine research, pooled labels written over substance-specific effects, and
treatment outcomes sometimes painted as recovery. ORAtlas is useful here because the graph can
keep those layers distinct instead of sanding them into one polished sentence.
