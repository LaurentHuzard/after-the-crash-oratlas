# Claim passports

This page provides a compact human-readable index. ORAtlas should use the JSONL artifacts as
the canonical graph.

(passport-c01)=
## C01 · Substance identity

**Question:** Is the studied substance explicit?  
**Main evidence:** [@asam2024; @li2023; @khalili2025; @sinclair2026]  
**Primary uncertainty:** ATS labels often conceal mixed or methamphetamine-only populations.

(passport-c02)=
## C02 · Evidence coverage

**Question:** How much direct non-methamphetamine amphetamine evidence exists?  
**Main evidence:** [@mcgregor2005; @zorick2010; @watson1972; @gossop1982; @cantwell1998; @oswald1963]  
**Primary uncertainty:** direct evidence exists but is often historical, small, or sleep-focused.

(passport-c03)=
## C03 · Acute phenotype

**Question:** What symptoms cluster during acute withdrawal?  
**Main evidence:** [@li2023; @mcgregor2005; @cantwell1998]  
**Primary uncertainty:** prominence and risk vary strongly across people and settings.

(passport-c04)=
## C04 · First-week trajectory

**Question:** Does withdrawal mostly resolve in week one?  
**Supporting evidence:** [@mcgregor2005; @zorick2010]  
**Disagreement edge:** [@gossop1982]  
**Primary uncertainty:** total scores and specific symptoms can follow different clocks.

(passport-c05)=
## C05 · Post-acute symptoms

**Question:** Which symptoms persist beyond the crash?  
**Main evidence:** [@zorick2010; @gossop1982; @oswald1963; @li2023]  
**Primary uncertainty:** prevalence, causality, and substance-specific duration.

(passport-c06)=
## C06 · Withdrawal medication

**Question:** Is any medication established for routine withdrawal treatment?  
**Main synthesis:** [@shoptaw2009; @acheson2023; @sinclair2026]  
**Disagreement edge:** [@srisurapanont1999b]  
**Primary uncertainty:** tiny trials cannot confidently prove benefit or no benefit.

(passport-c07)=
## C07 · Outcome separation

**Question:** Does reduced use imply symptom or functional recovery?  
**Main evidence:** [@trivedi2021; @mcketin2026; @sharafi2024]  
**Primary uncertainty:** trials measure different endpoints at different time scales.

(passport-c08)=
## C08 · Contingency management

**Question:** What does the strongest psychosocial evidence actually establish?  
**Main evidence:** [@asam2024; @decrescenzo2018]  
**Primary uncertainty:** stimulant-use outcomes do not directly answer acute-withdrawal questions.

(passport-c09)=
## C09 · Certainty of treatment evidence

**Question:** How robust are apparent medication signals?  
**Main evidence:** [@khalili2025; @sharafi2024; @bastien2025; @acheson2023]  
**Primary uncertainty:** heterogeneity, bias, small samples, and narrow outcomes.

(passport-c10)=
## C10 · Measurement portability

**Question:** Can one withdrawal scale travel across stimulants?  
**Main evidence:** [@walker2019; @srisurapanont1999a]  
**Primary uncertainty:** validation samples differ from target populations.

(passport-c11)=
## C11 · Psychiatric overlap

**Question:** How should withdrawal and co-occurring conditions be distinguished?  
**Main evidence:** [@asam2024; @sinclair2026; @li2023]  
**Primary uncertainty:** symptoms overlap and change at different rates.

(passport-c12)=
## C12 · Functional recovery

**Question:** Do studies measure rebuilding a workable life?  
**Main evidence:** [@khalili2025; @trivedi2021; @mcketin2026]  
**Primary uncertainty:** functional outcomes are sparse, secondary, or absent.
